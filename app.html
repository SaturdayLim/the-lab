What is FlavorGraph?

A graph of 8 000 ingredients and flavor compounds with 147 000 edges, built from two sources: statistical co-occurrence across more than one million recipes, and shared chemical affinity across ~1 500 flavor molecules. Each ingredient is mapped to a point in a 300-dimensional space — ingredients that taste good together sit close together.

What does the score mean?

The score (0–100) is the cosine similarity of ingredient embeddings — how close two ingredients are in flavor space. Higher = stronger, better-tested affinity.

90–100Well-tested. Classic, high-confidence pairings.
70–89Interesting territory. Worth exploring.
50–69Creative risk. Context-dependent.
< 50Adventurous. Proceed with curiosity.

The default threshold is 90. Drag it left to reveal more options. The yellow line on each score bar shows the current threshold.

Multi-ingredient queries

With multiple ingredients selected, the query uses the centroid — the mean of all your ingredient embeddings. Only flavours near the centre of your selection score highly. This means broad results for chicken alone, and focused results for chicken + chilli + garlic + onion. The more specific your palette, the more specific the suggestions. This is a feature, not a limitation.

Lock & unlock

🔒 Lock an ingredient to fix it in the query — it will always be included. 🔓 Unlock an ingredient to mark it as flexible; the Alternatives section will suggest what could replace it while working with the locked set.

If all ingredients are locked, Alternatives are hidden. New ingredients are locked by default.
